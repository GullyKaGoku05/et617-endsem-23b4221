Welcome to LaunchLab!

In this short lab you will launch virtual projectiles and find out for yourself how the launch angle and speed decide how far something travels.

💛 There are no wrong experiments here. Every launch, even an unexpected one, gives you useful evidence. Take it one step at a time.

🌍 What is projectile motion?

When you throw a ball, kick a football or fire a water jet, the object follows a curved path. It moves sideways at a steady speed while gravity pulls it down. The distance it covers along the ground is called the horizontal range.

🎯 By the end you will be able to

  1. Predict and test how launch angle changes the horizontal range.
  2. Explain how initial speed changes the range.
  3. Identify the angle that gives the maximum range.
